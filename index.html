About

From Atlanta to the helm

I grew up in Atlanta and have been a boater since childhood, with family places on Lake Lanier and on St. Catherines Sound on the Georgia coast, and I've always loved the sea. I take pride in my work and ownership of every job I do: from my very first job at McDonald's at 13 to captain of your vessel, you'll get my all.

Read my story

With the help of amazing mentors in yachting, I took my first command in 2025 and logged 4,290 nautical miles in my first year. My husband is part of my crew, and we work as a team. We turned our passion into a profession in 2023, hit the ground running, and put all of our knowledge and experience to work to move up quickly in the industry.

I have a passion for weather, which fits naturally with traveling the seas and keeping guests comfortable.

Before yachting, I ran businesses. After Covid I joined my husband in the printing business we own in north Atlanta. Before that I came back to my family's shoe store, where I did the buying and ran the business, after time in business and consulting. I started out in design: I studied it, and later went back to the same school to teach. Owning and managing businesses gives me a real understanding of budgets, planning and running an operation.
