Refit & yard management

From deferred maintenance to offshore-ready

When we took delivery in October 2025, she came with a long list of deferred maintenance. I brought her to offshore-ready for the run south, then oversaw an extended in-water yard period in Fort Lauderdale, working on nearly every system myself and using discernment to decide when to spend the owner's money.

  1. 01Oct – Nov 2025

    Offshore-ready

    Worked through the deferred maintenance to make her safe to take south.

  2. 02Winter 2025–26 · Fort Lauderdale

    Extended in-water yard period

    Oversaw the yard period and worked on nearly every system, using discernment to decide when to spend the owner's money.

  3. 03December 2026 · scheduled

    Engine and systems yard period

    Overseeing full rebuilds of both Caterpillar C12s, running-gear removal and service, air-conditioning work including air-handler replacements, and a new fuel manifold.
