Cruising Grounds

Captain's cruising portfolio · Nov 2025 – Oct 2026

The Bahamas to Down East Maine

A year in command of a 65′ Outer Reef motor yacht (private), from the Exuma Cays to Mount Desert Island. Every place here comes from my own log: harbors I have brought her into, beaches we anchored off, and the points of interest worth the detour for guests aboard.

Every one of these places was once a first visit. I take moving the boat seriously, and I plan each new passage with care. I have my own network of experienced friends to call on for local advice, and I'm confident in chart planning, reading the water and taking her somewhere new.

01 · Spring

02 · Summer

Coast of Maine

Casco Bay · Midcoast · Penobscot Bay · Mount Desert

Logged Jul – Aug 202618 harbors & towns4 points of interest

Portland to Mount Desert Island in high summer: Casco Bay, Boothbay, Penobscot Bay and Eggemoggin Reach. Fog, ledges, lobster pots and ten-foot tides are the daily working conditions, and the reward is some of the best cruising grounds on the East Coast.
